Staff logins

There is no public sign-up — only people you add here can sign in to the CMS.
  1. Open your Supabase project's Users page (button below).
  2. Click Add user, then enter their email and a password.
  3. They sign in at admin-login.html. To remove someone's access, delete them from the same list.

Roles & permissions

How access works right now.
Every account that can sign in currently has full access to everything in the CMS — content, media, messages and settings. Roles are not enforced yet, so only give a login to someone you would trust with the whole site.
The database already has a profiles table with super_admin and editor roles ready for this. Making them real (for example, editors can post News and Events but can't touch Settings or Messages) is a separate piece of work that can be added later.
