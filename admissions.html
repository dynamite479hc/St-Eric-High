Admissions

Join our community of learners — O-Level and A-Level.

O-Level Admissions
A-Level Admissions

O-Level Requirements

  • Completed application form
  • Copy of birth certificate
  • Previous school report(s)
  • 2 passport photos
  • Interview (if required)

O-Level Application Process

  • Submit the completed application form with all required documents
  • Attend an interview / assessment if invited
  • Receive confirmation of placement
  • Pay the confirmation deposit to secure the place

O-Level Fees

Tuition (per term)Contact school office
Registration feeContact school office

Exact figures are confirmed with the school office each term — please contact us for the current fee schedule.

A-Level Requirements

  • O-Level results (minimum 5 subjects, including Mathematics and English)
  • Completed application form
  • Copy of birth certificate
  • 2 passport photos

A-Level Application Process

  • Submit application with O-Level results and required documents
  • Select intended A-Level subject combination
  • Attend a subject-placement consultation
  • Pay the confirmation deposit to secure the place

A-Level Fees
